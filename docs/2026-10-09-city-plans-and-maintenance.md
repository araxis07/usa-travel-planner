# Six city plans and travel notice review — 2026-10-09

New York City, San Francisco, Las Vegas, Boston, Chicago and Philadelphia now offer the same editable 1–3 day trip preview. The button beside the city facts opens the native disclosure, scrolls below the sticky navigation and moves keyboard focus to its summary. Choosing a duration only changes the preview. Creating the trip uses the existing library's capacity and storage checks and preserves previous trips and their expenses.

Each day contains the published neighborhood visit and matching food stop, plus local travel and rest. All durations are planning allowances; start times remain suggestions until edited. Weather alternatives stay in the visit notes. Neighborhood visits remain unmapped until actual entrances are verified. Example budgets are not imported into expenses. No database connection or new dependency was added.
