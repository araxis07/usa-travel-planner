# Roam America v3.5 — 2026-09-22

This release completes the remaining destination access review and replaces generic photo captions, then makes arrival advice easier to find in the guide. No database was connected and no dependency was added.

## Changes

- The remaining 126 destinations have source-checked arrival guidance, named accommodation base areas and destination-specific official links in five languages. Together with v3.2's initial 24, this covers all 150 destination guides. See [the source inventory and review limits](v3.5-content-review.md).
- All 450 photographs were visually reviewed. The remaining 378 numbered captions were replaced with descriptions in EN / TH / ZH / JA / KO. Gallery captions and image alternatives identify the subject and clarify nearby or cross-border viewpoints. Original captions and attribution remain available in the lightbox.
- Two Florida-section images in the Mississippi guide were replaced with Horn Island and Davis Bayou photographs from NPS. Their new file URLs also bypass stale offline image caches; responsive variants and generated credits follow the new names.
- Arrival and accommodation guidance and official links appear before secondary walking/map references. Native expandable sections retain keyboard access, and a separate section groups broader state planning advice. Destination pages avoid repeating the large hero image in the photo journal. Existing overview facts and dated advisories remain visible.
- Refreshed the official NPS snapshot for 42 parks with 139 alerts, retaining the saved-data date, English-source label and stale-data reminder. Five newly curated access notices are visible before the guide body; routine reservation and entrance distinctions stay in arrival text.

## Validation

- All 122 regular browser checks passed across the suite and targeted reruns: desktop/mobile Chromium, targeted Firefox/WebKit and Android touch journeys, accessibility, Studio, planning, five-language captions, keyboard details and local backups.
- The first concurrent local run had two assertions against the previous location of the transport label and eight timeouts under host load. Updated the assertions to check the overview where that label now appears; all ten cases passed in a single-worker rerun without raising timeouts. The production offline case also timed out under concurrent load; the final separate production run passed all seven checks.
- Seven production checks passed, including a new upgrade scenario with old Mississippi photo cache entries, deferred data, responsive loading, no-JavaScript pages, reciprocal SEO metadata, offline images, print exports and mocked optional-account compatibility. No email or remote-database writes were performed.
- Content validation, TypeScript, the production build (1,005 localized pages), formatting and existing local PGlite compatibility checks passed.

## Screens and performance

Recorded production screens for the English desktop and Thai mobile home, state and place guides, expanded practical references, planner, budget, backups and landmark scenes. The recorded journeys had no page errors or horizontal overflow; visually inspected the mobile place guide and desktop practical section.

Three cold-cache local Chromium runs at 390 × 844, DPR 3, 4× CPU slowdown, 1.6 Mbps download / 750 Kbps upload and 150 ms latency, with service workers blocked. Builds and other test suites had finished before measurement.

## Review limits

The content review is editorial verification against primary sources, not an on-site survey or a guarantee of current opening conditions. Native-speaker sign-off, recruited usability sessions and physical iPhone/Android testing remain pending under [the existing protocol](usability-check.md). Their flags have not been marked complete. Automated browser emulation is not a physical-device or screen-reader test.
