## Budget assumptions and review

Every amount is a group allowance for **two adults, three days, two nights and one shared room**. Room, food, activity and reserve amounts are editorial choices, not researched market quotes. Transport references explain the assumptions, including individual JFK fares and the validity of RTC passes. Taxes, mandatory fees and tips have a separate estimated reserve; replace it with actual charges. Flights, insurance, entry documents, shopping and rental cars are excluded.
