## Boston and Chicago

Boston neighborhood references: [Downtown](https://www.meetboston.com/explore/neighborhoods/downtown-crossing-financial-district/), [Back Bay](https://www.meetboston.com/explore/neighborhoods/back-bay/), [North End](https://www.meetboston.com/explore/neighborhoods/north-end/). Day alternatives link to the [Central Library](https://www.bpl.org/locations/central/) and [Museum of Fine Arts](https://www.mfa.org/visit); check open days, holiday closures and tickets. Airport connections use [Meet Boston’s Logan guide](https://www.meetboston.com/plan/getting-here/logan-airport/). The indexed [Massport May 2026 announcement](https://www.massport.com/media/newsroom/massport-launch-first-its-kind-remote-terminal-june-1) confirms free SL1 travel from Logan to the city. Direct Massport access was blocked and the current [MBTA fare page](https://www.mbta.com/fares/subway-fares) denied crawler access, so **no numeric return fare is asserted**. The guide distinguishes paid return travel and Blue Line fares from free airport transfers.

Chicago neighborhood references: [Loop](https://www.choosechicago.com/neighborhoods/loop/), [River North](https://www.choosechicago.com/neighborhoods/river-north/), [South Loop](https://www.choosechicago.com/neighborhoods/south-loop/). The [city Cultural Center page](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/dca/supp_info/chicago_culturalcenter.html) supports free general admission. [CTA airport directions](https://www.transitchicago.com/airports/) support the Terminal 5 ATS → Terminal 2 transfer and Blue Line connection. The **published** [CTA fare chart](https://www.transitchicago.com/fares/default.aspx) lists O’Hare $5, regular rail $2.50 and a $15 adult 3-day pass with no additional O’Hare boarding charge; proposed budget increases were not used as enacted fares. [Blue Line alerts](https://www.transitchicago.com/blueline/default.aspx) must be checked for the actual journey.
