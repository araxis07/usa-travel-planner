## Decisions and limits

- Replaced Big Sur’s broad area pin with Big Sur Station (36.247463, -121.781755), using the [Coastwalk / California Coastal Trail primary access record](https://californiacoastaltrail.org/info-point/big-sur-station/). This is an information-center reference; it does not establish that the center or surrounding roads are currently open.
- Retained existing NPS visitor-center coordinates. Broad city, valley and island destinations retain area pins when a single entrance would misrepresent the destination. The catalog now contains 43 visitor-center references and 107 area references; the remaining area pins are visible in the Studio review queue.
- Read access pages for the initial 24 destinations and rewrote destination-specific arrival and onward-travel advice. Month tags are editorial planning preferences, not an opening calendar, weather forecast or guaranteed best season.
- California State Parks’ September 2, 2026 notice reports Big Sur park and Highway 1 closures. Its lower camping section contains conflicting older opening text. The app identifies the dated notice, links to the official page and avoids offering Big Sur in wizard recommendations until an editor clears the advisory. No automatic expiry is treated as proof of reopening.
- GoHawaii’s Kauaʻi page showed a travel-rescheduling advisory when checked on September 15. The banner does not show a publication date; the app explicitly distinguishes the observation date from a publication date. Follow the source for current guidance.
- Monument Valley sources contain inconsistent fee and hour text. No exact fee or opening time was copied into the edited advice.
- Replaced the Nankoweap granary image in the South Rim gallery with an NPS / M. Quinn winter view from Hermit Road on the South Rim. The original credit, public-domain terms and download URL are recorded in the catalog and generated credits.
- Niagara gallery captions identify Canadian viewpoints, including Skylon Tower. Hawaiʻi Volcanoes eruption photos are explicitly historical. The 72 captions describe visible subject matter rather than numbered placeholders.
- All five translation-review flags remain false. Photo caption review has separate per-language flags, editable in Studio and reset when that caption changes. The queue groups pending photo review by destination.
- Photo checks were visual checks of local assets and existing creator metadata; no original photographs were represented as current conditions.

## Follow-up editorial process

1. Open `npm run studio`, choose a destination in the review queue and verify its official access source.
2. Confirm the specific arrival point, parking approach, seasonal road issues and whether a saved advisory still applies. Update the checked and next-review dates only after checking.
3. Have a fluent/native reviewer assess each language and photo caption in context. Mark only the reviewed language; editing clears that language’s sign-off.
4. Save the draft, inspect its preview, then publish the local catalog revision. No database connection is required.
