# Export consistency and editorial review — October 20, 2026

## Itinerary exports

Text exports now use the same daily timeline as the planner: explicit and suggested start/end times, travel buffers between activities, and continuous trip-day numbers across states. They include localized checklists, planned expense totals and outstanding balances calculated per expense. An overpayment on one item does not hide an unpaid balance on another.

Text and print/PDF exports include each linked place's advisory once, every affected trip day, its checked date and official source. Readiness, text and print reuse the same notice selection. Print keeps section headings with their following content, shows the source URL on paper and includes the six default checklist items when no custom checklist exists; an explicitly empty checklist remains empty. Existing arrival references, notes, coordinates and attribution remain intact.

Notices come from the current bundled catalog, including on shared-trip print views; they are not a frozen part of the saved trip or a live opening-status service. Custom activity names are not inferred as places. No trip-storage format or data is changed.

The added five-language regression covers repeated visits, global day numbering, implied times reset each day, buffers, custom/default/empty checklists, per-item balances and unchanged saved data. The production offline scenario also downloads text and generates PDFs in all five languages.
