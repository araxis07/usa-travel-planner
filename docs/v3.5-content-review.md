# v3.5 destination and photo review — 2026-09-22

## Scope and method

Reviewed primary park, transport, operator and destination-management sources on September 21–22 for the remaining 126 destinations. Together with [the first 24 guides reviewed in v3.2](v3.2-content-review.md), all 150 have destination-specific arrival guidance. This round replaces broad state tourism links and generic access paragraphs for those 126, names possible accommodation base areas, and supplies EN / TH / ZH / JA / KO text. Base-area choices and suggested durations remain editorial judgments, not hotel or journey-time guarantees. Proper route, stop and neighborhood names remain searchable in their original spelling.

The review covers arrival routes, district selection, ferry/road distinctions, booking and closure caveats. It is not a fresh fact-check of every Wikipedia summary sentence. Broad area pins remain explicitly labeled as area references; the review does not turn them into precise parking coordinates. Location checked dates and summary licenses were retained. Guide review dates were updated only for the 126 records checked here, with the next review set for December 22, 2026. Temporary notices need checking sooner and remain dated rather than automatically implying reopening.

Refreshed the saved NPS dataset for 42 parks on September 21: 139 alerts, plus directions, hours and fee records. The UI continues to label this English source snapshot as saved information and flags it after seven days. Official links should be checked for current access; no exact price or all-year transit timetable is promised by the rewritten guidance.

## Important access decisions

- Denali's Pretty Rocks closure and seasonal road/bus limits are distinguished. Crater Lake's Cleetwood Cove and East Rim construction, Valley of Fire's seasonal and maintenance closures, Wichita Mountains' temporary heat restriction, and Apostle Islands' Little Sand Bay construction have dated, five-language notices visible above the guide. These five new advisory records follow the existing conservative wizard rule and are excluded from automatic suggestions until an editor clears the advisory.
- Mammoth Cave's tour reservations and Assateague's disconnected Maryland/Virginia entrances are visible in arrival guidance, without marking the whole destination as unavailable to the wizard. Boat-dependent visits at Kenai Fjords, Voyageurs, Ship Island and Apostle Islands are separated from mainland access. Juneau's lack of an external road connection and Mackinac Island's car-free arrival are explicit.
- The Des Moines paragraph points out that the old D-Line ended in November 2024, using [DART's service-change notice](https://www.ridedart.com/news-archive/dart-made-several-changes-services-november-24). Stowe's previous winter schedule is not presented as a confirmed upcoming timetable. Memphis guidance does not promise a currently running trolley. Kansas City's guide does not repeat the obsolete two-mile streetcar limit.
- Cape Henlopen's approach was cross-checked against the [Fort Miles visitor directions](https://fortmilesmuseum.org/plan-your-visit/); the park's own page is retained as the guide link. Hocking Hills uses the [local tourism authority's trail map](https://www.explorehockinghills.com/media/gdpl5ode/hockinghillstrailmap.pdf) and directions because the previous Ohio park URL was unavailable. Route 67 in Newport was checked against [the destination authority's trolley guide](https://www.discovernewport.org/blog/post/free-trolley/), without extending a seasonal free-fare offer beyond its dates.

## Photographs

Visually inspected all 450 local images in 25 contact sheets, checking visible subject matter against existing source captions and filenames. Retained the first 72 descriptive captions and replaced the remaining 378 numbered placeholders with five-language descriptions. Original creator captions and attribution remain separate in the lightbox. Caption text also supplies meaningful guide/lightbox image alternatives.

Two images in the Mississippi Gulf Islands guide showed the Florida section of the seashore. Replaced the old `ms-3.jpg` with `public/images/states/ms-3-horn-island.jpg`, an NPS / Kiss photograph of Horn Island, Mississippi, and the old `ms-2-3.jpg` with `public/images/places/ms-2-3-davis-bayou.jpg`, an NPS / Breault photograph of Davis Bayou, Ocean Springs. New asset URLs prevent previously saved offline photographs from overriding the corrections. Source pages, original download URLs, public-domain terms and dimensions are recorded in the catalog and generated photo credits. Inspected both replacements at full size.

Cross-border or nearby viewpoints are labeled explicitly rather than represented as entirely within the destination: Detroit from Windsor, Ontario; Cincinnati from Devou Park, Kentucky; South Lake Tahoe in California in the Nevada guide; Mabry Mill in Virginia in the North Carolina parkway guide; Inyan Kara in Wyoming in the Black Hills guide; and Geist Reservoir in Fishers in the Indianapolis gallery. Older event/vessel/stadium images retain historical context where supported. Rick Hall's accession number was not treated as a capture date, and a Denali reflection was not assigned an unverified lake name.

Photo review establishes editorial relevance, not current conditions or independent geolocation verification of every original. All translation and caption-review flags remain false until a fluent/native reviewer signs off. No recruited usability session, physical-device accessibility inspection or on-site visit is claimed.
